## What the reference site does

- Warm cream ground, near-black type, minimal chrome; product photography supplies the color.
- Uppercase, 1–3 word campaign headlines (`VELVETS`, `AFTER HOURS`, `FALL 2026`) against lowercase,
  dry supporting lines (`your winter uniform, sorted`).
- Product naming: `[Color] [Name] [Garment]` — `Espresso Mardana Kurta`.
- Editorial campaign layer on top of conventional commerce: mega-menu, quick-view, cart.
- Heavy social proof: looping Instagram grid, influencer tags.
- Quiet-luxury styling: neutral, tonal, strong per-product color stories.

## What we do differently

The reference is a mature store with a large catalog. Burgundy Atelier is smaller and sells by DM.
We should be **more editorial, less chrome**:

- Fewer navigation links, no mega-menu, no quick-view drawer at launch.
- No auto-looping Instagram grid — a static, curated row that links out.
- No cart until commerce is decided; a clear handoff instead.
- Slower, sparser pages — one idea per scroll on mobile.
